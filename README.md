Create a premium, multi-page website for Waris Brother Enterprises, a spices import/export company based in Vashi APMC Market, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

PAGES TO INCLUDE:

Home – Hero section with animated spice visuals, tagline, and CTA buttons ("Explore Products" / "Contact Us")
About Us – Company story, mission, vision, why choose us
Products – Full spice catalog with cards (use the product list provided)
Services – Import & Export services explained with icons
Why Us – Trust badges, quality certifications, global reach map
Contact – Contact form, Google Maps embed, phone, email, address


DESIGN & FEEL:

Style: Ultra-premium, luxury feel — dark rich tones (deep brown, gold, cream, saffron orange)
Typography: Elegant serif for headings + clean sans-serif for body
Animations: Smooth scroll animations, parallax hero, spice particles/floating elements, hover card effects, fade-in sections, animated counters (years of experience, countries served, products)
UI Elements: Glassmorphism cards, gradient overlays, golden dividers, cinematic full-width hero
Logo: Prominently placed in navbar and footer
Mobile Responsive: Fully responsive across all devices


TONE & CONTENT:

Professional, trustworthy, and globally oriented
Highlight APMC market credibility and bulk supply capability
Emphasize quality, authenticity, and direct sourcing of Indian spices
